Learn Bharatanatyam – India's Most Celebrated Classical Dance

Bharatanatyam is one of India's oldest and most respected classical dance forms, originating from the temples of Tamil Nadu. At Bhavayami Nrithalaya in Vazhuthacaud, Trivandrum, we offer comprehensive Bharatanatyam training for children aged 3 and above in a nurturing, child-friendly environment.

Our Bharatanatyam classes are designed to take students from their very first adavu (basic step) to complete mastery of this ancient art form. Whether your child is a complete beginner or has some dance experience, our structured curriculum ensures steady progress at every level.

What Your Child Will Learn

Our Bharatanatyam curriculum follows the traditional teaching methodology while incorporating modern pedagogical approaches that keep young learners engaged and motivated:

Foundation Level (Beginners)

  • Basic Adavus: The fundamental step sequences that form the building blocks of Bharatanatyam
  • Nritta (Pure Dance): Rhythmic movements without narrative content, building coordination and grace
  • Mudras (Hand Gestures): Learning the vocabulary of Bharatanatyam's expressive hand positions
  • Basic Rhythm Training: Understanding tala (rhythm cycles) through practice with nattuvangam
  • Posture and Body Alignment: Developing the characteristic araimandi (half-sit) position

Intermediate Level

  • Jathiswarams and Shabdams: Moving from individual adavus to complete dance compositions
  • Abhinaya (Expression): Learning the art of storytelling through facial expressions and body language
  • Varnam Preparation: Introduction to the most complex piece in the Bharatanatyam repertoire
  • Slokas and Keerthanas: Devotional compositions that deepen understanding of the dance form

Advanced Level & Arangetram Preparation

  • Full Margam (Repertoire): Mastering the complete sequence of a Bharatanatyam recital
  • Arangetram Coaching: Intensive preparation for the solo debut performance – a milestone in every dancer's journey
  • Stage Performance Training: Building confidence and stage presence for public performances
  • Theory and History: Understanding the rich cultural heritage and spiritual roots of Bharatanatyam

Benefits of Learning Bharatanatyam for Children

Enrolling your child in Bharatanatyam classes offers benefits that go far beyond dance:

  • Physical Fitness: Develops strength, flexibility, stamina, and coordination
  • Mental Discipline: Builds focus, concentration, and patience through structured practice
  • Cultural Connection: Deepens understanding of Indian heritage, mythology, and spiritual traditions
  • Confidence Building: Regular stage performances help overcome stage fright and build self-assurance
  • Academic Boost: Studies show that children who learn classical arts perform better in academics
  • Social Skills: Group classes and performances teach teamwork and collaboration
